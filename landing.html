The experience gap

Knowing the tools is one thing.
Delivering the engagement is another.

Courses teach concepts. CTFs sharpen exploitation. PentSim helps learners practice the complete professional workflow that often gets missed.

Not just flags and walkthroughs

Move beyond isolated challenges into realistic client objectives, testing boundaries, evidence requirements and decisions.

Practice the whole lifecycle

Learn to scope, plan, test, validate, assess impact, write findings and explain the results to a client.

Show evidence of your skills

Build a practical portfolio from assessed work—not just a list of courses, badges or tools you have tried.

One engagement at a time

From client brief to client-ready report.

Every mission follows a recognizable professional process. Start guided, then take on more independence as your skills grow.
